NBA Projection Model

Paper trading only. No real bets. The focus is game totals: v1 beat the opening total 55.2% in 2023-26 (1,785 bets with edge ≥ 3, +97.4 u at -110; the line then moved toward the model 63% of the time) but lost to the closing line, so plays are logged at the line when first flagged and judged by closing-line value. Spreads: v1 spreads tested with no edge (51.0% vs the open, 2021-26), so spread plays come from the injury-aware v3 (and v2), paper only and untestable on past data. Props have no edge.
Backtest 2023-26 (500k+ props): the market's line beat the model in every market, and real over rates track the market's price, not the model's. The lean column is the model's opinion only.
